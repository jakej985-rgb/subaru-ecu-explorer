## Identification checklist

- [ ] Record VIN privately and derive the exact model/trim; do not publish the full VIN.
- [ ] Photograph vehicle identification/build plate.
- [ ] Photograph ECU label and transcribe part number.
- [ ] Photograph ABS hydraulic/control unit label if accessible.
- [ ] Photograph diagnostic connectors before connecting anything.
- [ ] Record connector locations and wire colors from factory diagrams.
- [ ] Record warning-lamp behavior at key-on and while driving, if safe.

## Data handling

Do not commit full VINs, owner details, license plates, or other identifying information. Use a locally stored redacted vehicle identifier in captures and issue reports.
